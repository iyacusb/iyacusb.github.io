Me vs. Serdika traffic

I bike to work from Lozenets every morning. Most of it is fine. Pleasant, even.

Then there's the stretch around Serdika.

It's a survival game. Double-parked cars, delivery vans, pedestrians who've decided the bike lane is a suggestion, and taxis that change lanes based on vibes. Every morning I arrive with a little more adrenaline and a little less faith in humanity.

Score so far this year: me 1, Serdika 0. I'm still alive. That's a win.
