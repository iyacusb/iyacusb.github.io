In defence of NDK

People call the National Palace of Culture ugly. People are wrong.

NDK is brutalist and it doesn't apologise for it. It's a huge, honest block of concrete that says exactly what it is. No fake columns, no decorative nonsense. I respect that in a building. I respect that in code too, honestly.

Best angle, if you want my unsolicited advice: from the underpass side. You get the full weight of the thing leaning over you, and on a grey day it looks like the set of a very serious film.

Ugly? No. Misunderstood. Like most backend developers.
