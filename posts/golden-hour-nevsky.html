Golden hour at Alexander Nevsky

Lunch break, camera in the bag, ten minutes to spare.

Walked over to Alexander Nevsky Cathedral right as the light went gold. The domes basically glow at that hour. Tourists everywhere, of course, but if you wait a minute there's always a gap.

Got one frame I like. Ate my banitsa on the steps. Went back to work slightly calmer than I left.

Ten minutes of peace. Cheapest therapy in Sofia.
