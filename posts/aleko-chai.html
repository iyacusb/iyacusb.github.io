Aleko and the best chai in Bulgaria

Stressful week, so on Saturday I did the only sensible thing: walked uphill until I stopped caring.

Simeonovo up to Aleko hut, about 11 km round trip. Bug stayed home and sulked. My calves are now filing their own complaint.

The reward is the mountain chai at the hut, which is, and I say this as a man of measured opinions, the best chai in Bulgaria. Hot, sweet, served by someone who has clearly seen a thousand sweaty hikers and judges none of us.

Almost no phone signal up there. No Slack. I sat on the bench for an hour and nothing pinged.

Half-joking that I might move up there one day. Mostly joking. Like 70%.
