Paying for coffee with code I'm about to rewrite

Big news at Kodar Labs: we won the contract to rebuild Zrunche.

If you've bought coffee in Sofia since 2019 you've probably used it. It's the loyalty app where you collect little beans and eventually get a free flat white. About 40,000 people use it every day. I'm leading the backend.

So this week I sat in a cafe, paid for my coffee with Zrunche, then opened my laptop and started reading the code that just processed my payment. Very meta. Slightly terrifying.

First impressions of the legacy code: messy, undocumented, and full of modules nobody remembers writing. There's a file called final_v2_REAL.py. There's a function called do_stuff. It does a lot of stuff.

Week one of probably many. Pray for me.
